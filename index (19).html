Sample deliverable

An illustration of the format and level of detail of a written advisory report, using a fictional business and fictional figures.

Pricing and business economics review

Example client: a small artisan bakery (fictional)

Summary

The bakery sells its main product, an artisan loaf, at R40. After variable costs, each loaf contributes R16 towards fixed costs of R12,000 per month. The business is profitable, but its margin is thin and sensitive to small changes in volume.

750Loaves per month needed to break even
R2,400Monthly profit at 900 loaves
R16Contribution per loaf (R40 price less R24 cost)

Break-even analysis

Key findings

  1. Sales exceed break-even by only 150 loaves (20%), which leaves little protection against a slow month.
  2. A price of R44 would lift the contribution per loaf to R20 and reduce the break-even point to 600 loaves.
  3. If the higher price reduced sales by 10% (to 810 loaves), monthly profit would still rise from R2,400 to R4,200.

Recommendations

  1. Test a price of R44 for one month and compare actual sales with the 810-loaf scenario.
  2. Review the R24 variable cost per loaf, beginning with the largest ingredient supplier.
  3. Reassess the price if monthly sales fall below 700 loaves.

Illustrative example only. The business, figures and assumptions are fictional and do not represent a real client or a guaranteed result.
